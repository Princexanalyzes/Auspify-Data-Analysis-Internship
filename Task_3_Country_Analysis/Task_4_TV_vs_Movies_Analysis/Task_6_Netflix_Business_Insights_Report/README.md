# Task 6 (Advanced): Netflix Business Insights Report

## Project Overview
This capstone task presents an end-to-end Executive Business Intelligence analysis of the Netflix global catalog (8,790 records). Synthesizing findings across data preparation, geographical concentration, format segmentation, and runtime distribution, this project provides data-driven strategic recommendations for catalog optimization and subscriber retention.

---

## Strategic Business Findings & Executive Summary
1. **Catalog Composition & Format Economics:**
   - Feature films account for **69.6%** of the library, driving broad exploratory viewing.
   - TV Shows comprise **30.4%**, functioning as the primary retention engine through episodic engagement.
2. **Geographical Concentration Risk:**
   - The **United States** and **India** constitute the majority of production volume, highlighting the necessity to accelerate local-language originals across emerging European and Latin American markets.
3. **Maturity & Demographic Targeting:**
   - Over **45%** of the portfolio is categorized as Mature/Adult (`TV-MA`, `R`), underscoring Netflix's pivot toward high-concept, adult-skewing prestige drama.
4. **Content Lifecycle & Churn Control:**
   - Feature film duration centers around a median sweet spot of **98 minutes**.
   - Approximately **67%** of episodic series conclude after **Season 1**, reflecting a cost-conscious pilot pruning model.
